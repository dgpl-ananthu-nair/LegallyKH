What KSLU asked in 10 Law of General Contract papers from June 2019 to Jan/Feb 2026, topic by topic. Each paper has five units, and each unit has a 10-mark essay and a 6-mark short note or problem, both with an OR choice. Click a sitting to see that whole paper, click a topic to list every question asked on it, or hover over a square to see one question.

Essay, 10 marks Short note or problem, 6 marks Not asked

If a topic appeared as both an essay and a short note in the same paper, the square shows the essay. "Times asked" counts papers, not questions. Problems are filed under the rule they test, so the same facts can sit under two topics (for example, the coercion versus undue influence essay in Sept/Oct 2023). The October/November 2021 paper used the older pattern (any five 10-mark and any five 6-mark questions out of ten), so its questions have no OR choices. Unit numbers are the ones printed in each paper. Privity, contingent contracts, frustration and anticipatory breach move between units from one paper to the next, so each topic sits in the unit where it appears most often. Three papers carry two dates on the file (April/May 2022 is marked Dec. 2021, March/April 2023 is marked Dec. 2022, Sept/Oct 2023 is marked June 2023); the grid uses the main exam date. Some questions were checked against the scan itself because the text extraction was garbled.
