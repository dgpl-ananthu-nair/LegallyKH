# kslu-legal-studies

Study material for the 3-year LL.B. programme (KSLU), organised by paper.

## Contents

| Paper | Folder | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Family Law I (Hindu Law), Semester I, paper code 4004 | `family-law-1/` | Past question bank, 13 papers (June 2019 to Jan/Feb 2026) |

## Layout

```
kslu-legal-studies/
├── README.md
└── family-law-1/
    ├── question-bank.md
    └── question-map.html
```

`family-law-1/question-map.html` is the interactive Question Map (open in a browser): topics against the 13 sittings, with a per-paper view that lists questions by unit, essays first.

Each paper gets its own folder. Add new material (notes, case summaries, answer outlines) next to the question bank for that paper.

## Family Law I question bank

- 80 marks per paper; five Units, each with a 10-mark essay and a 6-mark short note or problem.
- Unit scheme: I Sources and schools · II Marriage and divorce · III Joint family, partition, Karta, endowments · IV Succession · V Adoption, guardianship, maintenance.
- Two of the 13 papers come from STS notes and could not be checked against a scan; they are marked † in the bank.
