Criminal Law I Question Map

What KSLU asked in four Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita papers from January 2025 to January 2026, topic by topic. Three are 100-mark papers (answer Q9, the problems, and any five of Q1 to Q8). The newest is an 80-mark paper with five units, each with an essay and a short note or problem, both with an OR choice. Click a sitting to see that whole paper, click a topic to list every question asked on it, or hover over a square to see one question.

Topics are grouped under the units of the new BNS syllabus. The 100-mark papers are not printed by unit, so their questions were placed by topic, using the 80-mark paper as the guide. If a topic appeared more than once in a sitting, the square shows the larger question. "Times asked" counts papers, not questions. Where one question covers two topics (for example, criminal conspiracy and how it differs from abetment), it appears under both. In the 100-mark papers Q8 asks for any two of three short notes and Q9 for any two of three problems. The file labelled "undated" is the January/February 2025 paper. This map covers four papers; older papers were not available, so a topic that has not appeared yet may still be asked.
